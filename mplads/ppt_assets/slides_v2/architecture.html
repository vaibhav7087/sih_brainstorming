3-Layer Anomaly Detection
Layer 1: 12 deterministic rules (MPLADS 2023). Layer 2: Isolation Forest for zero-day anomalies. Layer 3: XGBoost calibrated on 500+ CAG findings. ARI = max(Penalty, 0.55*XGB + 0.45*IF).
