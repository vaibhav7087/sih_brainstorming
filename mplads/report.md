## 1. Executive Summary

The Members of Parliament Local Area Development Scheme (MPLADS), launched on December 23, 1993, allocates each MP Rs 5 crore annually for constituency development. Across 85 reports, audits, sting operations, and investigations spanning 1993-2026, the following systemic issues emerge:

- **Chronic underutilization**: Only 37-52% of available funds spent annually
- **Massive unspent balances**: Rs 1,788 to Rs 12,000 crore at various points
- **Ghost assets**: 98.53% of works had no record of handover to user agencies
- **Bribery and corruption**: 18 MPs caught on camera in two 2005 sting operations
- **Political fund diversion**: Funds redirected to other states for party patronage
- **Incomplete works spanning decades**: Projects from 2002 still unfinished in 2024
- **Multiple bodies recommended abolition**: ARC, NAC, National Commission to Review the Constitution

- **Period Covered:** 2004-05 to 2008-09
- **Scope:** 128 District Authorities across 35 States/UTs

**Key Findings:**
- Rs 7,245.95 crore released; Rs 9,836.53 crore available (including opening balance of Rs 2,404.26 crore and interest of Rs 186.32 crore)
- Only Rs 8,048.53 crore spent; unspent balance: Rs 1,788 crore as of March 31, 2009
- Rs 73.76 crore spent on 2,340 prohibited works across 100 districts in 29 States/UTs
- Works worth Rs 9.45 crore executed without MP recommendation
- 305 incomplete works (Rs 8.5 crore) abandoned/suspended in 11 States/UTs
- 98.53% of 15,049 sampled works had no record of handing over to user agencies
- 57% of completed works not uploaded on MPLADS website
- Monitoring committees never met in 14 states/UTs; not constituted in 3 states
- Fund utilization only 37-52% annually
- 145 ineligible trusts/societies given funds in 7 states
- Rs 14.40 crore sanctioned for 34 trusts/societies exceeding Rs 25 lakh ceiling by Rs 5.90 crore
- 86 DAs of 23 States/UTs had not inspected any work during 2004-05 to 2008-09
- Basic internal control records (asset registers, works registers) missing in 90% of audited DAs
- 58% of Monthly Progress Reports with Ministry were more than two months old

**Amount of Money Involved:** Rs 7,245.95 crore released; Rs 1,788 crore unspent; Rs 73.76 crore on prohibited works

**Recommendations:**
- Establish reliable data capture system for releases, expenditure, unspent balances
- Strengthen internal controls and monitoring mechanisms
- Prescribe accountability for record maintenance at all levels
- Regular DA inspections of MPLADS works (especially Rs 5 lakh+)
- Robust internal audit system at ministry and state levels
- Upload all works to website after proper data validation

## 5. Supreme Court Judgment

### 5.1 Bhim Singh vs Union of India (May 6, 2010)

- **URL:** https://indiankanoon.org/doc/976795/
- **URL:** https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/Nothing-unconstitutional-about-MPLAD-scheme-rules-Supreme-Court/article16121962.ece
- **URL:** https://www.livelaw.in/know-the-law/mplads-why-sc-upheld-constitutionality-of-scheme-in-2010-154934
- **URL:** https://www.legalindia.com/news/mp-local-area-development-scheme-valid-apex-court
- **URL:** https://accountabilityindia.in/blog/sc-upholds-the-mplads-scheme-a-questionable-judgement-2/

**5-Judge Constitution Bench:** CJI K.G. Balakrishnan, Justices R.V. Raveendran, D.K. Jain, P. Sathasivam, J.M. Panchal

**Key Holdings:**
- MPLADS is intra vires the Constitution under Article 282 (public purpose)
- MP's role is purely "recommendatory" - execution by District Authorities
- "Mere allegation of misuse of funds by some MPs may not be grounds for scrapping"
- Three levels of accountability: (1) within Parliament, (2) Guidelines, (3) Annual Reports
- No violation of separation of powers - role is recommendatory
- No violation of 73rd/74th Amendments - PRIs are preferred implementing agencies
- Appropriation Acts constitute sufficient "law" under Article 282

**Dissenting Views:**
- Accountability Initiative: "SC gave legitimacy to a scheme that is fundamentally unconstitutional - a real blow to democracy"
- Second Administrative Reforms Commission recommended abolition
- National Commission to Review Constitution recommended "immediate discontinuance"
- Multiple MPs from all parties called for scrapping

**Amount of Money Involved:** Rs 2 crore per MP per year at time of judgment

## 7. RTI & Transparency Framework

### 7.1 RTI Wiki - MP/MLA Fund Utilization Guide

- **URL:** https://righttoinformation.wiki/rti-for-mp-mla-fund-utilization
- **URL:** https://righttoinformation.wiki/rti-mplads-mlalads-fund-utilization

**Key Findings:**
- Highest-signal fraud detection records:
  - Sanction orders vs market rates (cost inflation)
  - Payment vouchers (ghost vendors, duplicate payments)
  - Utilization certificates (fake/inflated UCs)
  - Physical progress reports (100% money spent but 40% progress = money leaked)
  - Inspection/audit reports
  - Beneficiary lists
- CIC (Sridhar Acharyulu) in 2018 directed MoSPI to disclose MP-wise, work-wise details
- Delhi HC in May 2024 upheld disclosure direction
- Section 4(1)(b)(xii) requires proactive publication of beneficiary lists
- Penalty of Rs 250/day (up to Rs 25,000) for unreasonable CPIO refusal

**Recommendations:**
- File RTI to District Collector for execution records AND to MoSPI for scheme-level data
- Cross-reference sanction orders with payment vouchers
- Use Section 2(j) inspection rights for large files
- Verify expenditure on ground: photograph site, compare with measurement books
